Privacy Policy

BizDesk ERP · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Summary

BizDesk ERP is an offline desktop application. Your business data stays on your computer and in your own Google Drive. We do not operate any server, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

Data stored on your computer

All records you enter (items, stock, orders, quotations, accounts, expenses, users) are stored locally on your computer in an encrypted file (AES-256). Only people with a valid BizDesk login on that computer can read it.

Google Drive access

If you choose to connect Google Drive, BizDesk ERP requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This permission only lets the app create and manage the files the app itself creates. BizDesk ERP cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

The app uses this access only to:

  • create a folder named "BizDesk ERP Backups" in your Drive,
  • upload encrypted backup files to that folder,
  • list, download (for restore) and delete old backup files in that folder.

Backup files are encrypted before upload, so their contents cannot be read by Google or anyone else without your BizDesk password. Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer, inside the encrypted database.

Use of Google user data

BizDesk ERP's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the backup feature described above.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in BizDesk ERP under Settings & Backup, or revoke access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the "BizDesk ERP Backups" folder removes all backups from your Drive.
